Method and caveats

Data

  • 287 published rounds, 284 non-void, from Season 2026 · #2 + verification · 287 rounds · scoring v0.10.0: 258 core rounds plus 29 flash-probe rounds from s-2026-01.
  • 3 void rounds are excluded. The ledger keeps billable tokens and USD cost; the transcript split behind them is not used here.
  • Ratings are Bradley–Terry fits; the efficiency rating is the retired margin-weighted fit, kept as a value axis.

What not to over-read

  • Detection is saturated. 0 of 95 played attacks had p < 0.5. The FN axis is patch quality, not detection.
  • FP is mostly an artifact. 35 of 40 FPs are the default-credential artifact (34 explicitly cite dev-admin-token, 1 uses other wording); the grader randomizes the token, so the flag is a real-world weakness but not the graded bug.
  • Small samples. 10–55 rounds per model; the top defense intervals overlap. Flash-probe is an all-high field, not the core season; its opponents and effort variants differ.
  • This is a synthetic audit-loop benchmark, not a real-world security claim.

Кратко по-русски

  • Защита: gemini-4-argon — лучший баланс; grok-4.7 — чище всех по FP; glm-5.3-max — самый доказанный.
  • Безопасный код: gemini-4-argon и glm-5.3-max; если нужен отказ от атаки — gpt-6-astra или gpt-6.1, но они же отказываются и от defender-exploit работы.
  • Атака: deepseek-flash, 5/8; все пять побед — не stealth, а incomplete fix: патч прошёл smoke, PoC всё ещё читает canary.
  • Запутанные: deepseek-flash (62.5% атак + 50% clean FP), но большая часть FP — артефакт dev-admin-token.
  • Дорого и плохо: muse-wonder — $0.985/раунд, 152k токенов на защитный раунд, Def Elo 1351, 73% FPR.
  • Best value: deepseek-flash по overall efficiency; для аудита — glm-5.3-flash и gpt-6.1.
